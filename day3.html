Momos Recipe

Maggi is a quick and tasty snack. It is easy to make at home.

Ingredients for veg momos

  • Maida (Flour) 2 cups
  • Cabbage,1 cup, finely chopped
  • Carrot : ½ cup, grated
  • Onion :½ cup, finely chopped
  • Garlic :1 tablespoon, chopped
  • Oil :1 tablespoon
  • Salt : As per taste
  • Black pepper : As per taste
  • Water: As needed for the dough

instructions

  1. Mix maida, salt, and water to make a smooth dough.
  2. Chop the cabbage, carrot, onion, and garlic.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and cook the vegetables with salt and pepper.
  4. Roll the dough into small, thin circles.
  5. Place the vegetable filling in the center of each circle.
  6. Fold and seal the edges to make momos.
  7. Steam the momos for 10-15 minutes until cooked.
  8. Serve hot with spicy red chutney.

Enjoy hot momos!
